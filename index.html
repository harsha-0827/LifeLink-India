LifeLink India is a digital platform designed to connect blood donors, patients, hospitals, blood banks, volunteers and healthcare organizations through a secure and user-friendly ecosystem.

Explore LifeLink India Learn More

Our Vision

To build India's most trusted digital platform that connects blood donors, healthcare organizations, volunteers and citizens to save lives while promoting legal and ethical donation practices.

LifeLink India aims to bring important donation-related services together in one secure platform.

Our Mission

  • Connect donors with patients quickly
  • Build a verified donor community
  • Increase donation awareness
  • Support hospitals and blood banks
  • Promote ethical donation practices

Important Donation Information

LifeLink India promotes awareness and legally compliant donation practices.

Organ Donation

LifeLink India will not arrange or match organ transplants. The platform focuses on awareness, explaining the legal process, donor pledge information, official government registration systems and authorized transplant-center information.
