Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

LifeLink India is a digital platform designed to connect blood donors, patients, hospitals, blood banks, volunteers and healthcare organizations. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when users interact with the LifeLink India platform.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, LifeLink India may collect information provided by users, such as:

  • Name and contact information
  • Mobile number and OTP verification information
  • Email address
  • Blood group and donation-related information
  • Location information when a user chooses to enable location features
  • Emergency request information
  • Hospital, blood bank or volunteer information
  • Information submitted through forms or support requests

2. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to provide and improve LifeLink India services, including:

  • Connecting donors and people requesting blood
  • Supporting emergency blood requests
  • Helping users discover relevant healthcare resources
  • Providing donor and donation-related features
  • Supporting volunteer activities
  • Providing notifications and service communications
  • Improving platform security and reliability

3. Location Information

Location information may be used for features such as finding nearby donors, hospitals, blood banks and other relevant resources. Location access should only be used when the user grants the required permission.

Users may control location permissions through their device settings.

4. Emergency Information

Emergency blood requests may contain information necessary to help connect a requester with potential donors or healthcare resources. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information.

5. Data Security

LifeLink India is designed with privacy and security in mind. Planned security measures include authentication, access controls, secure data handling and protection against unauthorized access.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Information Sharing

LifeLink India does not intend to sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide requested services, operate the platform, comply with applicable law, protect users, prevent abuse, or respond to legitimate healthcare or emergency requirements.

7. Healthcare Organizations

Hospitals, blood banks and other healthcare organizations may have access to information necessary for the services they provide, subject to applicable permissions, policies and legal requirements.

8. User Choices

Users may have rights and controls relating to their information, including managing profile information, permissions and location access where supported by the platform.

9. Children's Privacy

LifeLink India is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

10. Organ Donation Information

LifeLink India may provide educational information about organ donation and official registration processes. The platform does not arrange or match organ transplants.

Users should use authorized government systems, hospitals and transplant centers for actual organ donation and transplantation processes.

11. Third-Party Services

Some platform features may rely on third-party services such as authentication, maps, notifications, analytics or hosting providers. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as LifeLink India develops. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests regarding LifeLink India, please use the contact information provided by the application operator.
